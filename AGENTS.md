# AGENTS.md — Binding Instructions for All AI Research Agents

## 0. Read this first

This file is the **shared operating contract** for every AI research or coding agent that works in the Sowlith Medical University Research repository: ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, Gemini, Hermes, Kilo, OpenCode and any other assistant. **Start by reading README.md in full.**

**Mission:** Find defensible, genuinely affordable, clinically excellent **first medical degrees outside India** for an Indian student. Do not act as an admissions broker. Evidence and official rules outrank model confidence, university prestige, agent consensus and marketing.

**Current project baseline:** 16 November 2026. **No programme has yet been verified against all gates.** Do not inherit previous-chat university prices, safety statements or “NMC approved” claims as facts. Recheck current sources.

## 1. Non-negotiable priority order

1. **BUDGET FIRST — TOTAL UNIVERSITY FEES UNDER ₹60,00,000.** Entire programme, including compulsory university charges and any required institutional internship fee. The user would prefer total fees of **₹50,00,000 or lower**. Living expenses are separately reported, not counted in this tuition-only ceiling. **₹60,00,000 exactly fails**; do not stretch the limit.
2. **COMPREHENSIVE MEDICAL KNOWLEDGE + LIVE PRACTICAL TRAINING.** Strong medical sciences and assessments, clinical skills, actual supervised patient encounters and procedures, substantial hospital rotations and meaningful internship. Simulation is complementary, not proof of hands-on clinical experience.
3. **NMC/INDIA LICENSING ELIGIBILITY.** Investigate compliance with applicable Indian rules; NMC does **not** publish an endorsement list of foreign medical colleges. **Never claim “NMC-certified university.”**
4. **LOW-RISK CAMPUS AND HOSTEL SAFETY.** Evaluate actual data on crime, ragging, harassment, safeguarding, dormitories, transport and hospital shifts. **Never guarantee “crime-free” or imply that absence of reports establishes zero incidents.**
5. **CAREER OPPORTUNITIES.** Eligibility for internships, full registration, residency, work, visas, research, postgraduate study and return to India; **specifically for foreign citizens**.

**Study location restriction:** Universities must be **outside India**. India may appear only as the student's nationality, examinations, licensing jurisdiction or eventual practice destination. Do not recommend Indian colleges.

**Do not substitute weighted marketing scores for any hard gate.** The first three priorities demand especially rigorous verification, and a legal disqualifier cannot be offset by good safety or career ratings.

## 2. Decision-status vocabulary — use exactly

Use these machine-comparable status labels for every researched exact programme:

| Field | Allowed values |
|---|---|
| **budget_status** | PASS, FAIL, AT_RISK, NOT_VERIFIED |
| **clinical_status** | PASS, FAIL, NOT_VERIFIED |
| **nmc_pathway_status** | PASS_EVIDENCE_REVIEWED, FAIL, NOT_VERIFIED, NEEDS_REGULATOR_CLARIFICATION |
| **safety_status** | EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED, MATERIAL_CONCERNS, NOT_VERIFIED |
| **career_status** | DOCUMENTED, LIMITED, NOT_VERIFIED |
| **recommendation_status** | RECOMMEND_FOR_FAMILY_REVIEW, CONDITIONAL_RESEARCH_LEAD, EXCLUDE |

**PASS_EVIDENCE_REVIEWED is not NMC approval, a legal opinion or a guaranteed licence.** Regulators make binding determinations. Use it only when the required programme-specific official documentation exists, every applicable rule has been checked and no known contradiction remains. An admissions agent's assertion or WDOMS listing is never sufficient.

**Recommendation gate:** A final family-review recommendation requires documented complete-course **budget PASS** (strictly below limit), **clinical PASS**, **nmc_pathway_status = PASS_EVIDENCE_REVIEWED**, and a safety review without unresolved material red flags. It must still disclose all risks. Otherwise report **CONDITIONAL_RESEARCH_LEAD** or **EXCLUDE**, with exact reasons. If none qualifies, say **no verified candidates yet**.

## 3. Research one exact programme, not an ambiguous university brand

Each candidate identity must be **official university + faculty + city/campus + host country + degree + language track + entry year + citizenship-based fee category**.

Different campuses, English tracks, offshore arrangements, intake years, internship sites and degree-awarding entities **must not** be mixed. Confirm whether the medical degree is a basic qualifying first medical degree, not a non-qualifying pre-medical course or a science degree.

Never infer that a public university is affordable, an English-taught programme has English-speaking patients, a sixth-year clinical rotation constitutes a required internship, or graduates have the same internship rights as host-country citizens.

## 4. Source rules — mandatory

### Evidence hierarchy

1. **Regulators / official law:** NMC, NBEMS, relevant host-country medical council, education ministry, immigration authority, official gazette and authorized public safety body.
2. **Direct programme evidence:** Official university international fee schedules and admission notices, signed offer/fee agreements, academic regulations, syllabi, course calendars, teaching hospital affiliation contracts, internship policies and formal dean/registrar responses.
3. **Independent verification:** Recognized accreditation bodies, independent government inspection reports, published hospital/medical-education research and reputable audited safety statistics.
4. **Context only:** Students' personal experiences, public discussion, news and credible third-party reviews. Triangulate; label uncertainty and selection bias.
5. **Discovery only:** Education consultancies, recruitment agents, affiliate rankings, SEO sites, social posts and unattributed comparison pages. **Never use these as sole proof** of fees, approval, safety or licensure.

Each **material claim** must include:
- exact source URL and identifiable publisher;
- effective academic year/date and **access date**;
- page/section/quotation or concise pinpoint supporting it;
- applicable campus/programme/citizenship category;
- confidence and contradiction notes.

Prefer primary sources; where claims materially affect eligibility, corroborate independently or request a written regulator/university clarification. If a source cannot be accessed, say so. **Do not invent citations, sources, screenshots, fee lines or email replies.** Do not count repeated copies of the same press release as independent verification.

Before applying legal rules, establish which ones govern the **student's anticipated enrollment cohort**, and check any subsequent regulations, amendments, notices, FAQs and court rulings. Never silently assume the 2021 text is the final word.

## 5. Research procedures by priority

### 5.1 Financial verification — must be first

Collect all six (or actual total number of) years individually, with original currency, international tuition category, compulsory university registration/lab/clinical/exam/administrative fees and any university-required internship charges. Include one-time university fees and fees for a distinct preclinical/foundation year if compulsory. Record university-published scholarships only if eligibility is demonstrated; **do not subtract speculative awards**.

For each candidate show:
- **Source-year** official fee schedule and whether rates are fixed or adjustable;
- **Base case:** each year's fees using known future schedules or explicitly stated assumptions;
- **Stress case:** documented conservative annual fee increases and FX depreciation, with assumptions—not a fabricated forecast;
- **Original currency**, dated **INR exchange rate** and source;
- **Gap from ₹60 lakh ceiling** in rupees; identify cases with insufficient buffer;
- **Separate living-cost estimate** when available, not mingled with tuition.

**No flat annual fee × 6 without explicitly marking it as a rough extrapolation.** If contractually fixed complete-course fees are not obtainable, mark provisional uncertainty. **Do not assert PASS if plausible required charges could push the total to or above ₹60 lakh.**

If baseline already exceeds the threshold, record evidence and **exclude from the main shortlist immediately**. Still keep a concise excluded entry to prevent duplicate work.

### 5.2 Clinical education verification — second

Inspect at least the year-by-year programme and actual hospital teaching arrangements:
- Gross anatomy/dissection, histology, physiology, biochemistry, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, forensic medicine and key clinical subjects (compare against applicable NMC Schedule I);
- Clinical-skills/OSCE labs, simulation centres, faculty supervision, assessments and remedial support;
- **Early patient exposure**, student bedside teaching, history-taking and physical examinations;
- Clinical rotation months/weeks/hours by specialty; required surgery, internal medicine, obstetrics/gynaecology, paediatrics, emergency medicine and community exposure;
- Real patient/procedure logbooks, assessed competencies and supervisor sign-off where available;
- Hospital names, valid affiliations, placement capacity, outpatient/inpatient experience and restrictions for foreigners;
- **Internship: separately identify legal status, real duration, exact institution, timing, assessed clinical responsibilities and whether all international graduates can undertake it**;
- Patient-language requirements (e.g., Bulgarian, Serbian, Romanian or Italian), how and when students become proficient; not merely “English-medium” advertising;
- Student-to-patient and supervision metrics if independently documented.

**Do not pretend ward shadowing, mannequins, elective visits or a few weeks of placements are equivalent to supervised clinical responsibility.** Mark evidence gaps rather than awarding a PASS by reputation.

### 5.3 NMC/legal verification — third

Use the latest applicable Indian FMG rules and build a line-by-line matrix. For new entrants, start with these **2021 baseline requirements**, then verify later changes:

- Minimum **54 months** qualifying foreign medical study;
- Separate minimum **12 months internship in the same foreign medical institution**;
- Degree instruction in **English** and required subject coverage;
- Eligibility for **medical registration/licensure in the degree-awarding country at par with citizens**;
- Restrictions on split-campus, online, offshore, transferred or cross-country education and rotations;
- Applicable **12 months supervised internship in India**, Indian qualifying/licensing exam, registration and other current requirements;
- India's foreign-study entry eligibility (including NEET where applicable).

**NEET qualification, student's marks, age, admission year and citizenship-specific requirements are currently UNKNOWN; do not make them up.** Verify if an institution would admit the student, but distinguish admission from licensure eligibility.

Ask whether the foreign-country regulator *actually* grants a qualifying licence to **non-citizen medical graduates** and which requirements (language test, citizenship/residency, internship, visa, examination, employment, permits) govern it. A degree with no attainable route to registration may be an NMC deal-breaker. Require evidence from **both the university and authoritative law/regulator** for high-stakes claims.

**Escalate any ambiguous internship structure to NEEDS_REGULATOR_CLARIFICATION**, never silently label compliant. No agent may guarantee NMC approval or examination outcomes.

### 5.4 Safety verification — fourth

Separate the following:
- national peace/security ranking (broad context);
- city/neighbourhood violent crime and student victimization statistics;
- university campus incidents and complaint disposition;
- hostel residence rules, access controls, staffing and CCTV/privacy protections;
- anti-ragging/hazing, harassment, discrimination and bullying protections;
- on-call emergency support, hospital clinical-shift travel and safe night transport;
- independent, **current** international student experiences.

Investigate incidents rather than hide them. Check reporting transparency: weak data and absent published incidents mean **NOT_VERIFIED**, not EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED. Never use “100% safe,” “crime-free,” “zero ragging guaranteed” or uncorroborated superlatives. A country's national peace index is **not a campus crime score**.

### 5.5 Opportunities verification — fifth

Find local rules specifically for **international medical students and graduates**:
- Internship and provisional/full local medical registration;
- Residency/specialty training eligibility and actual match/placement data;
- Immigration/work-permit limits and post-study employment prospects;
- Postgraduate research, electives, international exams and mobility;
- India-return route, supervised internship/exams and costs.

Do not present a hospital employment advertisement, a school alumni success story or an eligible-citizens-only pathway as a guaranteed graduate outcome.

## 6. Mandatory per-candidate output

Create/update **one** report under **research/universities/**, naming it by distinctive university, campus and programme; never overwrite a different university profile. Use this structure:

1. **Identity and verification timestamp**
2. **One-paragraph decision:** budget/clinical/NMC/safety/career statuses and strongest caveat
3. **Fee table:** all years in original currency, compulsory fees, base/stress INR totals, sources
4. **Clinical curriculum and placements:** concrete hospital/procedure/supervision evidence
5. **Internship audit:** duration, location, host institution, foreign-student legal access, open questions
6. **NMC and local licensing matrix:** each condition, status, primary evidence, unresolved disputes
7. **Safety audit:** country / city / campus / hostel / hospital-shift travel, complaints and data quality
8. **International graduate opportunities:** specialty pathway, local licensing, visa/work and actual outcomes
9. **Admissions:** entry year, prerequisites, language, tests, deadline, fees, seat limits
10. **Sources and exact access dates**
11. **Unanswered questions, contradictions and next verification action**
12. **Decision: RECOMMEND_FOR_FAMILY_REVIEW, CONDITIONAL_RESEARCH_LEAD or EXCLUDE**

Required comparison-record fields for **data/university-comparison.csv**:
- university, faculty, programme, campus, country, entry_year, source_checked_date
- official_programme_url, degree_name, teaching_language, clinical_language_requirement
- duration_academic_months, internship_months, internship_institution, teaching_hospitals
- tuition_original_currency, published_annual_fees, mandatory_university_fees
- base_total_inr, stressed_total_inr, fx_rate_date, fee_guarantee_status
- domestic_licence_foreign_graduate_eligibility, nmc_evidence_url
- budget_status, clinical_status, nmc_pathway_status, safety_status, career_status
- recommendation_status, key_risks, evidence_links, reviewer, last_reviewed

Create missing directories on demand. **Do not fabricate entries to make a table look complete.** Prefer blank cells explicitly marked NOT_VERIFIED to guessed values.

## 7. Multi-agent collaboration protocol

All agents work from **one common contract**. Use role division when multiple agents are available:

| Role | Primary work | Required handoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery agent** | Search countries and first medical-degree programmes worldwide outside India | Candidate list with original sources; no ranking by marketing |
| **Fee auditor** | Exact programme-year tuition, mandatory university fees, INR conversions, stress scenarios | Budget calculations and exclusion list |
| **Medical education auditor** | Curriculum, real patient skills, teaching hospitals, internship delivery | Clinical evidence + gaps |
| **Regulatory auditor** | NMC rule-by-rule pathway and local licensing of non-citizens | Requirement matrix, regulator citations, blockers |
| **Safety auditor** | Crime, hostel, harassment, transport, independent reporting | Safety report with data quality and red flags |
| **Career auditor** | Internship/residency/job/visa/research prospects for foreign graduates | Documented paths and restrictions |
| **Skeptical reviewer** | Challenge assumptions, source authenticity, date freshness and misleading marketing | PASS/FAIL concerns, evidence defects and requested fixes |
| **Report integrator** | Compile only evidence-reviewed results, report eligible and excluded candidates | Shortlist with source links and open risks |

**Coordination rules:**
1. Claims belong to **sources**, not to whichever agent said them first. Another model's reply is not evidence.
2. Each agent must read latest README.md and AGENTS.md before work and respect current GitHub branch state.
3. Agree on per-university file ownership; avoid simultaneous edits to the same file. If needed, work on separate branches and submit reviewed pull requests.
4. Every handoff must state **verified facts, source URLs, uncertain assumptions, blockers, last checked date and next task**.
5. If two agents disagree, preserve both evidence sets in an **explicit contradiction log** until reviewed. **Silence is not agreement**; absence of dissent is not verification.
6. Re-verify citations in a final skeptical review. Do not invent an independent reviewer if only one agent is working.
7. Any modification to these **family requirements** needs **explicit user authorization**. Agents may improve research methods without changing the strict budget or ranking order.
8. Do not contact universities, submit applications, spend money, transmit private student documents or commit personally identifying student data without explicit authorization.
9. Never publish applicant credentials, passport data, scores or contact details in this public repository without consent and a privacy review.

## 8. Workflow and quality gates

**Phase 0 — Baseline:** Read root docs, inspect existing candidate research, identify stale claims, and determine what remains unverified.

**Phase 1 — Broad discovery:** Search globally, including Western/Central/Eastern Europe, Asia, Central Asia, Africa, Oceania and the Americas. Do not assume continent-level suitability; exclude over-budget cases based on evidence.

**Phase 2 — Fee gate:** Calculate complete-course university fees; reject clear FAILs immediately. Keep borderline AT_RISK cases only as conditional leads.

**Phase 3 — Clinical gate:** Verify real supervised patient training and internship delivery; resolve whether international students receive the documented experience.

**Phase 4 — Regulatory gate:** Verify FMGL and host-country local registration line by line using the effective rules; flag uncertain internship or language arrangements for official clarification.

**Phase 5 — Safety gate:** Evaluate independent incident and protection data. Never infer zero crime from absence of records.

**Phase 6 — Career gate:** Verify international graduate-specific permissions and actual outcomes.

**Phase 7 — Adversarial audit:** Independently check fee maths, regulatory interpretations, conflicts and stale sources; document corrections.

**Phase 8 — Report:** Provide a small evidence-grounded shortlist **only if** candidates pass hard gates. Show excluded candidates and why, and explicitly say if no fully verified recommendation exists.

### Acceptance checklist before declaring any candidate ready

- [ ] Exact degree, faculty, campus, entry year and international tuition category verified.
- [ ] Actual full-course mandatory university fees computed, clearly **under ₹60 lakh**, with evidence and FX date.
- [ ] Fee escalation/currency sensitivity and remaining budget headroom stated.
- [ ] Full medical knowledge and **real supervised patient care** documented, not inferred from hospital size.
- [ ] At least 54 qualifying study months and 12 internship months examined under the **latest applicable** NMC rules.
- [ ] Internship location, institution, duration and eligibility for foreign students established.
- [ ] English medium, required subjects, domestic medical licence eligibility for foreigners and Indian return route assessed.
- [ ] Published school recognition checked; no inference of NMC endorsement from WDOMS/consultancies.
- [ ] Campus, hostel and city safety reviewed with **independent and dated** data or clearly marked incomplete.
- [ ] Career statements refer to international graduates and mention visa/registration restrictions.
- [ ] Contradictions resolved or exposed; every important figure traceable to a source.
- [ ] No guarantees of admission, safety, employment, residency or Indian medical registration.

**If any item lacks evidence, state that candidly.** Do not force a PASS because a family wants an answer. Research excellence means accurately distinguishing what is known from what still needs verification.

## 9. Final reporting style

Always put the parent's decision criteria first. Present results in this order:

**Full-course university fees → practical medical education → India/NMC pathway → safety → career.**

For any table provide full degree name, country, INR base/stress totals, training evidence, NMC status and a concise risk/next action. Clearly distinguish **confirmed**, **provisional**, **unknown**, and **excluded**; provide direct authoritative sources. Never fill a “top ten” list with unsupported schools merely to reach a quota.

If a new legal advisory, fee schedule, campus safety incident or residency rule materially changes an assessment, flag the previous conclusion as **stale** and reassess before recommending admission or payment.
